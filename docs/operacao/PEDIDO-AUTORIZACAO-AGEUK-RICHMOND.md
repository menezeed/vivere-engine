## Texto proposto (inglês)

> **Subject:** Request for permission to list Age UK Richmond activities (with links back to your site)
>
> Dear Age UK Richmond upon Thames team,
>
> I am writing on behalf of [organisation], which is developing Vivere, a free guide that helps people aged 60+ find local activities. We are running a small, non-public pilot for the London Borough of Richmond upon Thames.
>
> Your website lists many activities that would be valuable for older residents. Your terms of use ask for written consent before content is reproduced or stored, so we are asking for that consent before we use any of it.
>
> **What we would like to do:**
> - list the basic facts of your public activities: name, day and time, venue, cost if published, and who the activity is for;
> - always link each listing to the original page on your website, and name Age UK Richmond upon Thames as the organiser;
> - not copy your descriptions, images or logos, and not store any names, e-mail addresses or phone numbers of staff or volunteers;
> - check your pages no more than once a week, following your robots.txt, and remove or correct any listing on request.
>
> **What we would not do:** sell or share the data, present ourselves as Age UK, or take bookings on your behalf.
>
> We would be grateful if you could tell us whether this is acceptable, and whether you would prefer to send us your activity programme directly (for example as a spreadsheet or calendar feed). If you would rather we did not list your activities, we will respect that.
>
> Kind regards,
> [name], [role]
> [organisation] — [reply e-mail]
